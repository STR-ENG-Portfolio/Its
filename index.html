Projects

Selected structural work, from classrooms to a control tower foundation.

01

4-Storey Classroom Building

DPWH Regional Office VIII · Educational

A 4-storey classroom building with a roof deck, budgeted at ₱80M. I handled the structural modeling and verification in ETABS, cross-referenced with STAAD.Pro to catch design anomalies. Soft-storey, mass and torsional irregularities and P-delta effects were all checked.

  • P-Δ stability index 0.028 (limit 0.10)
  • Mass ratio max 1.06 (limit 1.5)
  • ETABS
  • STAAD.Pro

02

School Auditorium

Tacloban · DPWH Regional Office VIII · Educational

One project budgeted at about ₱150M, in three parts. The hip truss of the third-floor sports court spans roughly 30 m × 45 m inside a 33 m × 71 m footprint, analyzed in STAAD.Pro. The mat foundation was designed in SAFE from the superstructure reactions. The 8.94 m front canopy rests on two columns and was modeled in ETABS.

  • Design wind 290 km/h
  • Canopy on an isolated footing with strap beam
  • STAAD.Pro
  • ETABS
  • SAFE

03

Mausoleum

General Trias, Cavite · Memorial

A reinforced concrete mausoleum about 4 m high with a roof slab and a front canopy. Designed to NSCP 2015 and ACI 318 for a 250 km/h design wind, seismic Zone 4 and an allowable soil bearing capacity of 143 kPa. Detailed with isolated footings, footing tie beams, columns, beams and a two-way roof slab.

  • Columns and beams 200 × 300 mm
  • Isolated footings 900 × 900 × 300 mm
  • NSCP 2015
  • ACI 318

04

EV Charging Stations

Region 8 · Infrastructure

Analysis and design of electric vehicle charging stations slated for construction at strategic locations across Region 8. Modeled in STAAD.Pro for a 310 km/h design wind, with framing and materials chosen for a standardized, economical design.

  • Design wind 310 km/h
  • STAAD.Pro

05

Steel Covered Court

Consolacion, Cebu · Community facility

Structural analysis and design of a steel covered court of about 17.5 m × 34.5 m. The steel framework was modeled in STAAD.Pro and detailed to local structural code requirements, with material efficiency and safety as the priorities.

  • STAAD.Pro

06

Hazardous Waste Warehouse and Office

Tacloban · Industrial / mixed-use

Foundation design for a 2-storey mixed-use building where a hazardous waste warehouse connects to a 2-storey office. Reactions from the superstructure model were imported into SAFE to design isolated footings, with strict settlement and deflection controls for long-term serviceability.

  • SAFE

07

3-Storey Mixed-Use Building

Guiuan · Commercial / residential

A 520 m² building with storage on the first and second floors and three rental rooms on the third. STAAD.Pro analysis was cross-referenced with ETABS, RCDC handled member design and detailing, and the structural plans were produced in Revit.

  • Floor area 520 m²
  • STAAD.Pro
  • ETABS
  • RCDC
  • Revit

08

Air Traffic Control Tower Foundation

Catbalogan, Samar · Aviation infrastructure

Foundation design for an air traffic control tower with a foundation area of about 14 m × 40 m. Superstructure reactions from the STAAD.Pro analysis were the basis for the manual design of the foundation system.

  • STAAD.Pro

09

2-Storey Villa

Siargao · Hospitality

A premium rental villa for a resort, with a distinctive roof truss system that carries both its architectural identity and its structural performance. Engineered for wind speeds up to 310 km/h and designed with the Equivalent Static seismic method.

  • Floor area 96 m²
  • STAAD.Pro

10

Residential and Mixed-Use Buildings

Palo · Tacloban · Frame analysis

Four frames analyzed in STAAD.Pro: a 160 m² two-storey residential, a 200 m² two-storey mixed-use with an open-plan ground floor of about 10 × 10 m, a 176 m² two-storey residential in Palo, and a 150 m² three-storey residential in Tacloban. ETABS cross-verified the results and RCDC designed and detailed members and foundations on selected buildings.

  • STAAD.Pro
  • ETABS
  • RCDC

Also completed: a 3-storey resort residential building, an administration building foundation, covered parking and a bungalow house.
